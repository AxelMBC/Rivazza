## REMOVED Requirements

### Requirement: Braking-point markers revealed for the focused lap
**Reason**: Braking ticks are short marks struck across the driven line at a fixed screen length — visually the same object as a sector boundary, and drawn in the same place. Observed in the running app: with both present the driver read the braking ticks *as* the sector division, and because braking points move from lap to lap while sector boundaries do not, the fixed division appeared to drift between laps. That is precisely the confusion the track sector division exists to remove. Two overlapping sets of marks also reproduce the "visual noise" this requirement was itself written to prevent. Braking onsets remain legible — more precisely than a tick ever made them — in the analysis panel's pedal strip, which plots them against track position for the selected lap and the reference lap together, and in the map lines' own pedal gradient, which already renders braking in red.

**Migration**: None for users: the braking-point ticks stop rendering, the analysis panel's pedal trace is where braking points are read, and the map's lines keep their pedal-colored gradient. In code, `computeBrakeTicks`, `drawBrakeTicks`, the `BrakeTick` type, the `brakes` field on the map's stored-lap entries and the `BRAKE_ON` / `BRAKE_OFF` / `BRAKE_REARM_M` / `BRAKE_TICK_*` constants are deleted. Every other cue on a focused lap — the emphasis stroke, cut markers, hover readout and scrub ring — is unchanged.
